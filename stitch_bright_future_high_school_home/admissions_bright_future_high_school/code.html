Frequently Asked Questions

When is the application deadline for 2025?

expand_more
The priority application deadline for the Fall 2025 semester is March 15th, 2025. Rolling admissions will continue thereafter until all seats are filled.

Are there academic scholarships available?

expand_more
Yes, Bright Future High School offers Merit Scholarships for top-performing students and Financial Need grants. Over 30% of our student body receives some form of financial assistance.

What is the average class size at BFHS?

expand_more
To maintain academic excellence, we maintain a student-to-teacher ratio of 12:1. The average class size is approximately 18 students.
